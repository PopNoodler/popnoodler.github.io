Cold Commander 1991

Role: Programmer  ·  Language: C++ & Blueprint Visual Scripting  ·  Engine: Unreal Engine 4

Developed during a game jam for the Imperial War Museum, this retro-themed simulation recreates Cold War scenarios in 1991. Players manage US Air Force operations, deploying aircraft strategically to maintain global stability.

What I built

  • An objective system requiring selected aircraft to meet specific criteria.
  • Aircraft dispatch and movement to destinations.
