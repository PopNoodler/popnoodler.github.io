The Game

Beer In The Sun

A free one-button arcade game about the great Scottish ritual: drag your deckchair into the one gap in the clouds, chug your pint before the rain gets it, pour your own at the Buzy Bee, and balance a full pint on your palm all the way hame over the rooftops.

Works on your phone. Traffic never hurts you. Scotland does.

Splash Hero

A fast, juicy firefighter tycoon. Spray the fires, dodge the flames, ride the truck out, then upgrade your HQ until you're putting out the whole solar system.

On iPhone: open in Safari and Add to Home Screen to play fullscreen and offline.
