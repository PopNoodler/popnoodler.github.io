Games

Beer In The Sun

A one-button arcade game about the great Scottish ritual: drag your deckchair into the one gap in the clouds, chug your pint before the rain gets it, pour your own at the Buzy Bee, and balance a full pint all the way hame over the rooftops.

I made it as a fan tribute to Graeme "GMCG" McGuigan and his song Beer In The Sun — hear the real thing on Spotify.

Water Hero

A fast, juicy firefighter tycoon. Spray the fires, dodge the flames, ride the truck out, then upgrade your HQ until you're putting out the whole solar system.
